# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

JAGGAD Academy serves Indonesian students, professionals, freelancers, and entrepreneurs who want practical digital and business skills they can apply to study, career, freelance work, or business growth.

Admins operate the platform through a CMS to manage products, categories, site content, ads, users, payment methods, settings, and transactions.

## Product Purpose

JAGGAD Academy is a digital education commerce platform for discovering, buying, and accessing learning products including ebooks, video classes, webinars, and offline classes.

Success means learners can understand the offer, purchase with confidence, complete payment, and access their learning materials, while the team can manage content and commerce without deploying code.

## Positioning

JAGGAD Academy focuses on practical, flexible learning from practitioners, bridging the gap between academic learning and fast-changing digital industry needs in Indonesia.

## Operating Context

Public visitors browse the home page, product catalog, product detail pages, promotional pages, package landing pages, about page, and contact page.

Authenticated customers use checkout, verify payment, manage their profile, and access purchased learning products from their dashboard.

Admins use the backend dashboard to manage catalog data, content, ads, users, transactions, payment methods, and integration settings.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The product is built as a Laravel 12 web app with React 18 and Inertia.js, Vite, custom CSS, session authentication, Google OAuth, Midtrans payment integration, Meta Pixel and Conversions API, and admin-managed integration credentials.

Payments support Midtrans automation and manual payment methods. Production is documented for MySQL; local development may use SQLite.

Core terminology to preserve includes produk, kategori, checkout, dashboard, kelas, ebook, video kelas, webinar, kelas offline, transaksi, pembayaran, and admin.

## Brand Commitments

Preserve the JAGGAD Academy name, Indonesian-language product copy, red brand direction, and admin-managed settings model.

## Evidence on Hand

Repository evidence includes README setup and deployment documentation, seeded product/category/site content, Laravel routes, public React pages, user dashboard pages, admin pages, and payment/auth controllers.

No real testimonials, customer logos, press quotes, performance claims, or external proof assets are confirmed beyond repo sample data; future work must not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

Make the purchase path clear from product discovery through payment verification and learning access.

Keep content and commerce manageable from the admin CMS without code changes.

Prefer practical, outcome-oriented learning language over generic education claims.

Preserve Indonesian context, terminology, and buyer expectations.

Do not invent proof, credentials, or business claims that are not present in product evidence.
