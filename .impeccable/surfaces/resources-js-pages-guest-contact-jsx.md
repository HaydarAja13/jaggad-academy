## Scope

Public Contact route (`/contact`), Persuade mode.

## Visitor

Indonesian learners, buyers, and potential collaborators who need a clear human route before choosing a program, resolving a purchase/access issue, or discussing a partnership.

## Job and action

Understand within seconds that the JAGGAD team can help, then start a WhatsApp conversation or open a prepared email message. Direct contact details remain available without hunting.

## Content and constraints

Use only admin-managed contact details and honest product language. Do not invent response times, testimonials, statistics, or guarantees. All marketing copy, topic descriptions, form copy, CTA labels, and contact labels stay editable in Admin Content.

## Direction

Inherit the About page's cool-white ground, deep-maroon mass, bold Chillax display type, Synonym body copy, 16px corners, and pill actions. The memorable moment is the first-viewport pairing of an oversized question with one decisive maroon direct-contact panel. Tablet and mobile stack copy, CTA, and contact panel in that order.

The surface roll used seed `ec40c6c8`. The user explicitly declined generated comps and directed a code-led implementation grounded in the existing About page.

## Unresolved

The message form intentionally opens the visitor's email client; add server-side message delivery only when an inbox workflow and recipient ownership are defined. Production contact details must be verified by the site owner in Admin Content before launch.
