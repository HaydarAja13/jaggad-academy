# Product sales and purchase handoff

- Scope: `ProductSales` purchase surface; Persuade mode.
- Audience and job: an Indonesian customer who has already shown purchase intent and needs to understand the product, contents, exact price, and next step without distraction.
- Primary action: review the offer, confirm the order, then continue to the existing checkout.
- Product evidence: product name, description, price, image, benefits, curriculum, FAQ, countdown, and campaign blocks all come from admin-managed product data; do not invent testimonials, scarcity, guarantees, or outcomes.
- Direction: a calm consultation desk in the established JAGGAD public world—cool white reading field, editorial hierarchy, structured dividers, and one deep-maroon purchase mass.
- Memorable moment: the first viewport pairs the full product promise with one image-led offer panel where price and purchase action are immediately visible.
- Constraints: Chillax for headings only, Synonym for body and controls, 16px minimum public text, 44px minimum targets, semantic reading order preserved on tablet and mobile.
- CMS connection: product facts come from menu Produk; landing blocks, FAQ, urgency, and confirmation copy remain editable through CMS Konten and render through the same ProductSales component used by its preview.
- Unresolved decisions: none.
